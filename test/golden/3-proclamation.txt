We, Isolde the Lightly Buttered, Esquire (in exile), Queen of Calderwick (in
  pretence), Keeper of the Privy Seal, Lord High Wielder of the Whisk, Warden
  of the Cruet, Master of the Otterhounds, Lady of the Order of the Golden
  Fleece, Dame Commander of the Order of the Golden Marten, the Medal of
  Valour, &c., &c., &c., to all to whom these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Calderwick, this twenty-eighth day of March, in the year
  of Our Lord 1596.
