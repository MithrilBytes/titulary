We, Godgifu the Punctual, Esquire (in exile), Queen of Wexcombe (in pretence),
  Master of the Horse, Hereditary Keeper of the Draught Excluder, Warden of
  the Decorative Soaps, Hereditary Grand Falconer, Lady of the Order of the
  Garter, Dame Commander of the Order of the Wittgenau Lion, the Cross of
  Merit, &c., &c., &c., to all to whom these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Wexcombe, this twenty-eighth day of March, in the year
  of Our Lord 1596.
