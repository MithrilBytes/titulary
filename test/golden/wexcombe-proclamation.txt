We, Æthelred the Just, of the House of Hautmont, Lord of the Manor of
  Brackenholm, Hereditary Chief Butler, Under-Falconer to the Margravial Mews,
  Master of the Buckhounds, Clerk of the Doilies, Knight of the Order of the
  Amaranth, Companion of the Order of the Second Drawer, the Medal of Valour,
  &c., &c., &c., to all to whom these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Brackenholm, this sixteenth day of November, in the year
  of Our Lord 1791.
