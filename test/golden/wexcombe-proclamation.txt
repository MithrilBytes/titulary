We, Æthelred the Black, of the House of Malassis-Boisjoli, Lord of the Manor
  of Netherleigh, Pinder, Under-Warden of the Lesser Gate, Hereditary
  Arch-Huntsman to the Empire, Wielder of the Feather Duster (State
  Occasions), Companion of the Order of the Iron Crown, Companion with
  Instructions (Lost) of the Order of the Deckchair, &c., &c., &c., to all to
  whom these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Netherleigh, this sixteenth day of November, in the year
  of Our Lord 1791.
