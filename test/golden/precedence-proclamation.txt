We, Sigeric XI the Insufficiently Boiled, of the House of Drayhithe-Marlcombe,
  by the Grace of God and the Rota, Grand Duke of Ravensmere-juxta-Hesselholm,
  Archduke of Garsthorpe-juxta-Ravensstadt (in exile), Earl of Selhurst,
  Viscount of the Wapentake of the Picnic Hamper (claim lodged), Baron of
  Osmundwick (by courtesy), Lord Proprietor of the Greater Immersion Heater
  (in abeyance), Thane of Lower Draygrange, Laird of Bexholm-cum-Crowmarsh
  (vacant; claimed), Lord Warden of the Cinque Ports, Master of the Great
  Wardrobe, Master of the Egg Timer, Hereditary Bearer of the Oven Gloves,
  Protector of the Lesser Forests, Defender of the Faith, Knight of the Order
  of the Crescent, Knight Commander of the Order of the Overwound Clock, the
  Coronation Medal, &c., &c., &c., to all to whom these Presents shall come,
  Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Ravensmere-juxta-Hesselholm, this fourteenth day of
  September, in the ninth year of Our Reign.
