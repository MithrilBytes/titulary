We, Roderick XI the Insufficiently Boiled, of the House of Marau-Greyhurst, by
  the Grace of God and a Narrow Vote, Grand Duke of the Upper and Nether
  Marches, Archduke of Hither Norbourne and the Palatinate of Hesselmouth (in
  exile), Marquess of Isestadt, Earl of the Airing-Cupboard Palatinate (by
  courtesy), Viscount of Ravensmere (claimed), Baron of the Lesser Vestibule
  (dormant), Laird of Upper Marau, Lord of the Manor of Wychbourne (vacant;
  claimed), Lord High Admiral, Warden of the Marches, Hereditary Keeper of the
  Draught Excluder, Lord High Steward, Advocate of the Abbey of Wittgenau,
  Defender of the Faith, Knight of the Order of the Garter, Knight Commander
  with Collar and Receipt of the Order of the Slightly Bent Fork, &c., &c.,
  &c., to all to whom these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at the Upper and Nether Marches, this fourteenth day of
  September, in the ninth year of Our Reign.
