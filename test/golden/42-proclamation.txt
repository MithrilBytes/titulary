We, Petronilla the Easily Startled, of the House of Osmundwick-Marstadt, Lady
  of Greater Wychbourne and the March of Caldberg, de jure Lady of the Manor
  of the Damp Marches, Hereditary Chief Butler, Hereditary Cup-Bearer to the
  Electoral Court, Under-Falconer to the Margravial Mews, Comptroller of the
  Biscuit, Lady of the Order of the Amaranth, Dame Commander of the Order of
  the Starry Girdle, &c., &c., &c., to all to whom these Presents shall come,
  Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Greater Wychbourne and the March of Caldberg, this
  twenty-first day of June, in the year of Our Lord 1615.
