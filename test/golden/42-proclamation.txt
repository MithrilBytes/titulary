We, Ælfthryth the Vaguely Remembered, of the House of Crowmarsh-Drayhurst,
  Thane of the Honour of Chalkcroft, de jure Lady of the Soke of the Doilies,
  titular Lady of the Manor of Montfaucon-le-Bas-cum-Bexbourne, Hayward,
  Hereditary Cup-Bearer to the Electoral Court, Keeper of the Hourglasses,
  Surveyor of the Middle Distance, Lady of the Order of the Porcupine, Dame
  Commander of the Order of the Winter Sun, &c., &c., &c., to all to whom
  these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at the Honour of Chalkcroft, this twenty-first day of June,
  in the year of Our Lord 1615.
