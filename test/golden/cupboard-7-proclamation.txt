We, Godwine the Great, of the House of Harrowfield-Isewick, titular Duke of
  Marlingford, Marquess of Greyhurst, Earl of the Greater Boot Room (claimed),
  Viscount of the Lesser Egg Cup (in pretence), Baron of Nether Dunsmere,
  Baronet of Lower Wexcombe (by courtesy), titular Laird of
  Schleswig-Netherleigh-Caldberg, Lord of the Manor of Ravensmere (in
  pretence), Warden of the Marches, Lord High Admiral, Under-Falconer to the
  Margravial Mews, Grand Master of the Hunt (Suspended), Companion of the
  Order of the Falcon of Hohenfelsen, Companion with Collar and Receipt of the
  Order of the Damp, the Cross of Merit, &c., &c., &c., to all to whom these
  Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Marlingford, this twenty-fifth day of April, in the year
  of Our Lord 1440.
