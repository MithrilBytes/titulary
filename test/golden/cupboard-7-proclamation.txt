We, Wulfstan the Just, of the House of Mickleover Green-Thornstadt, titular
  Marquess of Snodbury, Earl of Quernmouth, Viscount of the Lower Tablecloth
  (claimed), Baron of the Greater Water Butt (in pretence), Lord Proprietor of
  Outer Doverow, Chief of the Name of Greater Calderwick (claim lodged),
  titular Laird of Schleswig-Emberwick-Falworth, Lord of the Manor of
  Thornbury (in pretence), Master of the Great Wardrobe, Hereditary Chief
  Butler, Master of the Pigeon Post, Hereditary Warden of the Eel Weirs,
  Companion of the Order of the Silver Bough, Companion with Both Keys of the
  Order of the Filing Cabinet, the Medal for Zeal, &c., &c., &c., to all to
  whom these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Snodbury, this twenty-fifth day of April, in the year of
  Our Lord 1440.
