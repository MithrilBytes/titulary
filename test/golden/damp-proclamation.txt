We, Æthelburga the Magnificent, Countess of Nether Doilies, de jure
  Marchioness of Netherleigh, Viscountess of Old Thornbury and the Palatinate
  of Ostershire, Baroness of Lower Dunburg, de jure Baronetess of Ravenswick,
  Dame of Aldenmouth (vacant; claimed), Lady of Brackenholm (in pretence),
  Lady of the Manor of Marford (by courtesy), Hereditary Cup-Bearer to the
  Electoral Court, Master of the Buckhounds, Hereditary Keeper of the Spoons,
  Hereditary Chief Butler, Lady of the Order of the Crescent, Companion with
  Collar and Receipt of the Order of the Slightly Bent Fork, the Medal of
  Valour, &c., &c., &c., to all to whom these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Nether Doilies, this sixteenth day of October, in the
  year of Our Lord 1363.
