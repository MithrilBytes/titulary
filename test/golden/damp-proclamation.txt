We, Æthelburga the Scholar, Baroness of the Damp Marches, de jure Countess of
  Hollowmere, Lady Proprietrix of Saxe-Fernycroft-Wamble, Chief of the Name of
  Nether Grimstein, de jure Baronetess of Nutstadt, Dame of Bramdorf (vacant;
  contested), Thane of Dunsmere (in pretence), Lady of the Manor of Draycombe
  (claimed), Hereditary Cup-Bearer to the Electoral Court, Hereditary
  Arch-Huntsman to the Empire, Hereditary Keeper of the Draught Excluder,
  Pinder, Lady of the Order of the Amaranth, Companion of the Order of Merit
  of the Pantry, the Household Order of the Butter Dish, Third Class, &c.,
  &c., &c., to all to whom these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at the Damp Marches, this sixteenth day of October, in the
  year of Our Lord 1363.
