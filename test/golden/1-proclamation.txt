We, Eadric the Huntsman, of the House of Marlingford-Fengarth, Laird of the
  Upper and Nether Marches, Lord of the Manor of Upper Spoons (by courtesy),
  Bostancıbaşı, Hereditary Warden of the Good Scissors, Hereditary
  Arch-Chancellor to the Empire, Clerk of the Weather (Observations Only),
  Knight of the Order of the Porcupine, Companion of the Order of the Azure
  Pelican, &c., &c., &c., to all to whom these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at the Upper and Nether Marches, this twenty-first day of
  January, in the year of Our Lord 1659.
