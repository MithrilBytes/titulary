We, Æthelstan the Silent, of the House of Ravensmere-Nordorf, Lord of the
  Manor of the Two Aldbournes, Clerk of the Green Cloth, Warden of the Lesser
  Stairwell, Lord High Steward, Under-Falconer to the Margravial Mews, Knight
  of the Order of the Amaranth, Companion of the Order of the Wittgenau Lion,
  &c., &c., &c., to all to whom these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at the Two Aldbournes, this twenty-first day of January, in
  the year of Our Lord 1659.
