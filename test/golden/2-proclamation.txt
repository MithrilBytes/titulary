We, Eadgyth, of the House of Wexburg-Thornbury, Lady of the Manor of Dunberg,
  Lord High Adjuster of the Thermostat, Hereditary Chief Butler, Lord High
  Rearranger of the Hall Cupboard, Hereditary Remembrancer of the Spare Keys,
  Lady of the Order of the Amaranth, Dame Commander of the Order of the Tea
  Cosy, the Medal of the Damp, Second Class, &c., &c., &c., to all to whom
  these Presents shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Dunberg, this twenty-seventh day of May, in the year of
  Our Lord 1536.
