We, Wulfrun, of the House of Yarstein-Fernycroft, Lady of the Manor of
  Grimsett, Keeper of the Takeaway Menus, Pinder, Warden of the Letter Box
  (Inward), Keeper of the Filing Cabinet (Bottom Drawer Sticks), Lady of the
  Order of the Broom-Flower, the Perfect Attendance Medal, Dame Commander of
  the Order of the Notice Board, &c., &c., &c., to all to whom these Presents
  shall come, Greeting.

Given at Our Court at Grimsett, this twenty-seventh day of May, in the year of
  Our Lord 1536.
